Historic Renovation

Honoring the narrative of historic structures by reimagining their utility for the modern era—transforming legacy assets into vibrant centers of civic life.

513 Rugby Road (1985–1986)

Location: Charlottesville, VA

Year: 1985–1986

Program: Fraternity house restoration (Pi Kappa Alpha)

While serving as President of his fraternity in 1985-86, Graham organized a partnership to plan and renovate the chapter house - “Memorial Hall” of Pi Kappa Alpha Fraternity.

303 Venning Street (1996)

Location: Mount Pleasant, SC (Old Village)

Year: 1996

Program: Historic renovation & preservation

Moving from Beaufort to Mt. Pleasant in 1996, Graham purchased and renovated this 1,200 square foot home at the corner of Pitt and Venning Street in the heart of Mt. Pleasant’s historic “Old Village.” The property contains a live oak planted by an elementary school class on Arbor Day in the 1920s, and named “George Washington.” A second tree (“Martha”) was planted nearby. Both trees have grown to immense size. Graham said he bought a tree and a house came with it.

Fort Sullivan

Location: Sullivan's Island, SC

Year: c. 1891 (renovated 2010)

Fort Sullivan is a small beach castle on Sullivan’s Island at the foot of Fort Moultrie National Historic Park. Comprised of a c. 1891 former church (the Chapel) and c. 2010 addition (the Mugdock Keep), it was designed as a comfortable bastion of hospitality for family and friends to enjoy time at the beach.

St. James

Location: Charleston, SC

Year: c. 1858

Program: Event Venue, Café, Residences

Coming soon! The historic c.1858 St. James Methodist Church—most recently home to the Karpeles Manuscript Library Museum—is being fully restored to create a vibrant new destination in the heart of downtown Charleston, featuring an elegant event venue, neighborhood café, and five thoughtfully designed short-term rental residences.

414 Whilden

Location: Mount Pleasant, SC

Program: Artist Galleries

Embedded in the Earl’s Court neighborhood, this unassuming commercial building was fully renovated to house two vibrant artist galleries.

Beulah (2021)

Location: Edisto Island, SC

Year: 2021

Program: Fractional-ownership historic estate restoration

In 2021, Vince Graham, with partners Reid Burgess and Sally Eisenberg, led the development and restoration of a 19th century historic property adjacent to Botany Bay Wildlife Management Preserve on Edisto Island, South Carolina. Using a fractional-ownership model, 11 stewardship-minded families including themselves, own this extraordinary property and share in its use.
