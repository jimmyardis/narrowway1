New Neighborhoods

Creating places of enduring value through the design and development of walkable, human-scaled neighborhoods that foster connection and relationship.

Newpoint

Situated on a high bluff overlooking the Beaufort River, Newpoint was the earliest of Graham’s place-making endeavors. Advancing the maxim “they had it right the first time,” Newpoint takes inspiration from the charm of Beaufort’s historic district with intimate, tree-lined streets, front-porch architecture, and memorable parks. Newpoint established an exemplary model for coastal civic design.

I’On

Located in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, I’On is a nationally recognized model of traditional neighborhood design. Founded on the principles of classic place-making, it integrates diverse residential architecture with vibrant public spaces and a village square, creating a timeless Lowcountry neighborhood that prioritizes the pedestrian over the automobile.

Hammond's Ferry

A 200-acre neighborhood designed to seamlessly connect the city of North Augusta to its roots along the Savannah River. This project represents a commitment to riverfront revitalization through walkable, mixed-use planning.

Lead Developer: Leyland.

East Beach

A 90-acre waterfront community in Norfolk, Virginia designed and built in the tradition of Atlantic Coastal villages. This development showcases the scalability of traditional neighborhood design along the Chesapeake Bay.

Lead Developer: Leyland and Frye Properties.

Las Catalinas

A thoughtfully planned, car-free town on Costa Rica’s Guanacaste coast. The over 12,000-acre town was designed to foster walkability, community connection, and harmonious integration with the surrounding tropical landscape. Vince Graham participated as a minor investor.

Lead Developer: Charles Brewer.

Catfiddle Street

An innovative infill development located in downtown Charleston, South Carolina. It exemplifies how to successfully integrate new housing into a historic urban fabric with a focus on intimacy and human scale.

Lead Developer: Reid Burgess.
